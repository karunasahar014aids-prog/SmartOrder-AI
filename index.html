FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers

How do I track my order?

Enter your order ID in the support flow. In this demo, use SO-2026-1042 to see a sample shipped status.

Can I return an item?

Yes. Returns can be requested within the eligible return period. Keep the order ID and reason ready for support.

When will my refund arrive?

After approval, refund timing depends on the payment method. If an amount was already deducted, keep your transaction reference available.

What if the bot cannot solve my issue?

Select Human Support. The issue summary is prepared for a support representative without asking for unnecessary sensitive data.
